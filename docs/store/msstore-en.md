## Product name

Rebels of Gaming

## Short description

The official Windows app of the multigaming clan Rebels of Gaming: forum, direct messages, clan events, game servers
with live status and Minecraft stats – all on your PC. No ads, no tracking.

## Description

Rebels of Gaming is a multigaming clan since 2020 – from Enemy Territory and Counter-Strike to Minecraft. The official
Windows app brings the whole clan to your PC: read, write, make plans and jump straight onto the server. With
everything a desktop does better – multiple windows, Windows notifications, a tray icon, keyboard shortcuts,
drag & drop and the clipboard.

FORUM AND NEWS
• Forum with boards, threads, polls and reactions
• Reply with formatting, quotes and images – even offline, sent as soon as you’re back online
• Read, comment on and share clan news

MESSAGES
• Real-time direct messages
• Any conversation in its own window if you like, optionally always on top – handy next to your game
• Friends, online status and who’s in TeamSpeak or Discord voice right now

EVENTS
• Clan nights, matches and tournaments as a list and month view
• RSVP yes, maybe or no, see participants and line-ups
• Add events to your calendar

GAME SERVERS AND STATS
• Our game servers live: status, players, load over the last 24 hours
• One-click “Connect” (Steam, Enemy Territory) or copy the address
• Minecraft: how to join (Java and Bedrock), world map with live players, leaderboards, lobby secrets and your stats
  on your profile – only if you choose to share them
• Squads, games, matches and results

NOTIFICATIONS
• Replies, messages and events as Windows notifications
• Taskbar badge, start in the notification area (can be turned off), jump list
• You decide what you get

PROFILE AND COMMUNITY
• Your profile with games, squads and achievements
• Upload images by drag & drop, Ctrl+V or “Share” from other apps; gallery
• Read, search and edit the wiki, also offline
• Applications, votes, absences, member map (only with consent and only to city level)
• Browse the shop & donations and track your own orders – purchases happen on the website

FOR SQUAD LEADERS AND ADMINS
• Moderation, member and application management, news editor, events and matches
• Server control in its own window: start/stop, live console, configs, backups, RCON and uploads with four-eyes
  approval
• Protected by Windows Hello

YOUR DATA
No ads, no tracking, no analytics. You sign in in your browser – with Discord or email and password – and your sign-in
token is stored encrypted in Windows Credential Manager. Without an account you can look around as a guest. The app
is available in German, English and French and is open source (GPL-3.0).
